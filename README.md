# India Digital Ad Tracker

Who advertises most on Google and Meta in India, in which industries, with which ad formats, and what it costs.

**Live dashboard:** https://yashyadav1997.github.io/india-digital-ad-tracker/

Open [index.html](index.html) in any browser. It is a single file: no server, build step, library or API key. The only outside request is the Figtree font from Google Fonts; offline it falls back to a system font. Instagram Sans is used only if it is already installed.

**Data snapshot: 26 November 2026.** The page does not update itself.

## What is in it

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | Headline leaders and top advertisers on each platform |
| Companies | Sortable table with links to check each figure live |
| Industries | Ad counts summed by industry, with coverage |
| Company and industry pages | Click any name; each page takes an approximate brand or industry colour |
| Compare | Up to four companies side by side |
| Instagram & funnel | Instagram placement share and button intent from sampled ads (needs the sample file below) |
| Ad types | Google text, image and video mix; Meta creative formats and buttons |
| Cost & revenue | Disclosed ad expense beside company revenue; platform and retail-media ad revenue |
| Political ads | Google's published political ad spend |
| ROAS lab | Scenario calculator for return on ad spend |
| Method & sources | How each number was obtained, its limits, and source links |

You can filter by industry or company, download the table as CSV, and switch light or dark theme.

## Selected findings

These describe a hand-picked sample of 190 companies in 16 industries, not the whole Indian market or a verified ranking.

- **Google (all dates, ranges):** Amazon India 700,000–800,000 ads, Booking.com 200,000–300,000, MakeMyTrip and Agoda 100,000–200,000, then Goibibo and MagicBricks 50,000–60,000.
- **Meta (active on the snapshot date):** Flipkart 9,615, Meesho 5,720, Nykaa 3,963, Purplle 3,538, IndiaMART 2,310.
- **Typical company on Meta:** Beauty & Personal Care has the highest median, 504 active ads.
- **Disclosed ad expense (all media):** Swiggy ₹4,207 cr (FY26), Eternal ₹3,350 cr (FY26), Myntra ₹2,105 cr (FY25), Flipkart Internet ₹1,586 cr (FY25).
- **Ad expense as a share of revenue:** Honasa 36.0%, Myntra 34.8%, Swiggy 18.2% (FY26), Nykaa 12.1%, Flipkart Internet 7.7%.
- **Political ads on Google since February 2019:** ₹949.7 cr across 404,310 ads. The Bharatiya Janata Party is the largest advertiser, with 25.9% of spend.

## What this does not show

- **Revenue generated by ads.** No company publishes it. Revenue beside ad cost is a ratio, not return on ad spend.
- **Commercial ad spend per platform.** Ad counts are not money.
- **A like-for-like platform comparison.** The Google count covers all dates and the Meta count covers active ads. Do not add them.
- **The whole market.** FMCG, usually among the largest advertisers, is missing from the rankings. A partial FMCG view is shown separately in the Industries tab.

## Data coverage and quality

| Data | Coverage | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Google total ads | 186 of 190 companies (4 had none found) | Ranges; counts every account linking to the website; a few use a global website |
| Google format split | All three formats for 6 companies | Earlier pass over the first 53 only; some counts look inconsistent |
| Meta active ads | 160 of 190 companies | One page per company, read through a third-party data service |
| FMCG | Google 5 of 14, Meta 7 of 14 | Counted by advertiser account and one brand page, so not comparable and not ranked |
| Finances | 21 company-year rows | Mostly press reports of filings, not re-read from the filings |
| Platform ad revenue | Google India only | No verifiable Meta India figure, so none is shown |

## Refreshing the data

**Google counts**

1. Open <https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=IN>.
2. Open the browser console and paste [scripts/collect_google_console.js](scripts/collect_google_console.js).
3. Keep the tab in front. It pauses between pages and stops if Google refuses requests.
4. In the dashboard, choose **Load newer data (JSON)** and pick the downloaded file.

The script has not been run end to end in its current form, and its website list covers only the first 53 companies. An import lasts until you reload the page.

**Instagram and button samples**

Choose **Load newer data** and select all the saved Meta lookup result files (`.txt`) together. The page summarises them, shows the Instagram view and downloads `meta_samples.csv`, which can be loaded again later. `scripts/extract_meta_samples.ps1` does the same from a terminal where scripts are allowed; it has not been run. The raw lookup results are not part of this repository.

**Meta counts, finances and other figures** are updated by editing the data block in `index.html` (search for `DATA SNAPSHOT`).

Review Google's and Meta's terms before collecting data regularly or using it commercially.
